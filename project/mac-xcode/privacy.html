Privacy Policy for Boxedwine on macOS

Last updated: October 18, 2026

Boxedwine is an open-source project maintained by James Bryant. This policy covers Boxedwine on macOS, with separate information below for versions distributed outside the Mac App Store.

Mac App Store version

The Mac App Store version has no advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs, or Boxedwine accounts. Boxedwine does not send app-usage telemetry or upload your library to the project. Requests you choose to make to a website may be logged by that website, as described below.

Wine and Wine’s Minesweeper are included with the app. Importing your own software from local files, ZIPs, or folders takes place on your Mac and does not contact Boxedwine’s servers. You can also import a collection from a local JSON file or URL, then choose apps to download and install. Collections may download online icons and can be refreshed manually, as described below. No demo collection is included by default, and the app does not download additional Wine packages. Windows software you run may make its own network connections.

Your apps and files

Boxedwine stores your app library, imported Windows files, saved games, settings, and launch logs on your Mac. It does not upload these files or your library to us. Launch logs can contain file paths, program arguments, and output from Windows software.

Backups are saved to the location you choose. Permanently deleting an app in Boxedwine removes its Windows files from the library. Backups and original files stored elsewhere remain until you delete them separately.

Downloads you choose

In versions that offer URL downloads, Boxedwine connects to the host specified by the download link you enter or the app listing you choose. A host may redirect the download to another host. Each contacted host receives your IP address, the URL requested from it, and standard request headers, and may record this information under its own privacy policy. A URL may identify the app, game, or collection requested and may contain information or access tokens included in the link.

Boxedwine does not route third-party downloads through its servers or send a separate report of those downloads to the project. The downloader does not use browser cookies, saved web credentials, or a Boxedwine account identifier. If a download contacts boxedwine.org, including through a link you enter or a redirect, the website hosting and logs described below apply.

Imported collections

In versions that support collections, imported listings are stored on your Mac, including their names, descriptions, download URLs, icon URLs, app settings, and the source URLs used to import the collection. Importing a collection from a URL downloads the collection file from that host; the app and game packages are downloaded only when you choose to install them. Collections imported from either local files or URLs may reference online icons. Boxedwine downloads uncached icons in the background when saved listings are loaded, including after relaunch. These requests contact the icon hosts and follow the download practices described above. Downloaded icons are cached on your Mac for reuse, including offline.

Collections remain available after relaunch. Their listings do not refresh automatically. Choosing Refresh Collection downloads each saved source URL again so you can review changes before applying them; importing a URL again also makes another request to its host. Deleting a collection removes its saved listings and source URLs. Reset Demos removes custom additions, overrides, and their source URLs. These actions keep installed apps, their saves, and the shared icon cache, and do not delete records held by download hosts.

Windows software you run

Windows apps and games run through Boxedwine may access the network, the shared clipboard, and folders you authorize. They may communicate with their publishers or other services. Their own privacy practices also apply; this policy describes Boxedwine’s handling of data.

Support and your choices

If you open a website link from Boxedwine, such as this privacy policy or the GitHub issue tracker, your browser contacts that site. Its hosting logs and privacy practices apply. Visits to boxedwine.org use the hosting described below.

Boxedwine does not automatically send launch logs to the project. If you contact us, we receive the information you choose to send and use it to respond and resolve your request. Review logs and screenshots before sharing them. Issues posted on GitHub are public.

Support correspondence is kept as needed to address your request. To ask about your information or request deletion of information held by the project, email boxedwine@danoon.net. Information controlled by another service is subject to that service’s policies.

Apple diagnostics and beta testing

Apple handles App Store diagnostics under its own privacy settings and policies. If you test a beta through TestFlight, Apple shares testing information with the developer, including crash reports, usage information, tester details, and feedback you submit. We use this information to troubleshoot and improve Boxedwine. See Apple’s TestFlight privacy information.

Optional tips

The Mac App Store version offers optional one-time tips through Apple’s in-app purchase system. All features remain free. Apple processes the payment under its own privacy policy; Boxedwine does not receive your payment-card details. The app checks and finishes transactions through StoreKit on your Mac and does not send your purchase history to Boxedwine’s servers. Apple provides sales and financial reports to the developer.

Versions outside the Mac App Store

GitHub and Jenkins builds offer Wine and bundled-demo downloads from boxedwine.org over HTTPS. The bundled demo list and its icons are included in those builds. User-provided URLs and imported collections can point to other hosts; those requests follow the download practices described above.

Boxedwine website hosting and logs

Our website host, DreamHost, records access and error logs for visits and downloads that reach boxedwine.org, regardless of which app or browser makes the request. These can include your IP address, the requested file or URL, request time, response status, and standard request headers such as the user agent. A download URL can identify the app, game, collection, or Wine package requested. These hosting records are separate from information stored locally by the app.

DreamHost provides traffic reports based on these logs, including request counts, device categories, and approximate locations derived from IP addresses. This information supports hosting, troubleshooting, security, and understanding traffic. DreamHost documents a default retention period of seven days for raw HTTP logs. We do not sell these records or use them for targeted advertising. See DreamHost traffic reports and DreamHost’s privacy practices.

For privacy questions, contact boxedwine@danoon.net. We will update this page when these practices change.
